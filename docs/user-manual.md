## 1. What 411 Scanner Does

411 Scanner investigates suspicious messages, ads, websites, and offers — the kind that show up in your social feeds, texts, and emails — and tells you what's really going on.

Point it at something fishy: a "too good to be true" crypto ad, a stranger asking for money, a website you've never heard of. The scanner researches it with AI, checks live sources, and gives you a plain-language report with a score. You'll know whether it's legitimate, a hustle, or outright dangerous — before you click, install, or send anyone a dime.

---

## 2. How to Scan

1. **Open the app** and sign in (or continue as a guest for your free weekly scan).
2. **Capture the suspicious thing** — take a screenshot or photo of the message, ad, or website. Upload it in the app.
3. **Free users:** watch one short ad all the way through. That's what pays for your free scan.
4. **Get your report.** Read the score first, then the details.

Each scan uses **one credit** from your plan — whether it's a brand-new investigation or one we've already done for someone else. (Reusing past investigations is how we keep prices low.)

---

## 3. Understanding Your Score

Your report comes with a score. Here's what it means:

| Score | What it means | What you get |
|-------|---------------|--------------|
| **0 – 5.5** | Looks legitimate | Verified contact information for the real source |
| **5.6 – 7** | Proceed with caution | A "fog light" — comparable, legitimate alternatives to consider instead |
| **8** | Do not trust it | Do not install it or put it on your device |
| **9** | Report it | Report to the platform (Facebook, Play Store) and the FCC |
| **10** | Dangerous | Malware or outright dangerous — stay away |

A low score is good news. A high score is the scanner doing its job: keeping you safe.

## 6. Deep Dive

A Deep Dive is a deeper, second-level investigation of a scan you've already run. It costs **1 top-up credit** — not a subscription scan.

- You need a top-up balance to deep dive. No credits, no dive — the app will offer you a top-up pack instead.
- Family members deep dive from the shared family bank.
- If the deep dive can't complete, you'll see an error code (see Section 9).

## 8. If Something Goes Wrong — Error Codes

When a scan can't complete, you'll see a plain-language explanation and a **5-digit reference code**. Quote the code if you contact support — it tells us exactly what happened.

### 1xxxx — Connection (your phone can't reach us)
- **10101** — Your phone isn't connected to the internet. Check your connection and try again.
- **10102** — The connection dropped while sending your scan. Try again.

### 2xxxx — Scanner engine (our backend stumbled)
- **20101** — The scanner was still warming up. Wait a moment and try again.
- **20102** — The scanner is busy right now. Try again shortly.
- **20201** — The scanner hit an unexpected problem on our end. We've logged it — try again.
- **20301** — The investigation took too long and timed out. Try again.

### 3xxxx — AI research (the investigation couldn't finish)
- **30101** — Our research AI declined this target. Not everything can be investigated.
- **30201** — Our safety systems blocked this investigation.

### 4xxxx — Scans & billing
- **40101** — You're out of scans on this plan. Top up or upgrade to keep going.
- **40201** — Deep dives need a top-up credit. Pick up a top-up pack to unlock this.
- **40301** — We couldn't verify that purchase. If you were charged, contact support.

### 5xxxx — Your submission
- **50101** — That doesn't look like a link we can scan. Check it and try again.
- **50201** — That image is too large to scan. Try a smaller one.

### 6xxxx — Free scan & ads
- **60101** — You've used this week's free scan. Come back next week, or pick a plan.
- **60102** — The ad didn't finish playing. Watch it all the way through for your free scan.

### 7xxxx — Deep dive
- **70101** — The deep dive ran into a problem. Try again.

### 8xxxx — Sign-in
- **80101** — Your sign-in expired. Sign in again to continue.

### 9xxxx — Unknown
- **90101** — Something unexpected happened. We've logged it — try again.

## 9. Managing Your Subscription

- **Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel** anytime through the Google Play Store: Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
- Canceling stops future billing. Your scans remain available until the end of the current billing period.
- Top-up credits are yours forever, even if you cancel — they never expire.
- Refunds follow Google Play's standard refund policy.

## 10. Your Privacy

- Scans you submit are used to perform the investigation and to improve detection for everyone (repeat scams get caught faster).
- We do not sell your personal information.
- Your sign-in is handled by Google; we never see your Google password.
- Account deletion is available — contact support and we'll remove your data.
