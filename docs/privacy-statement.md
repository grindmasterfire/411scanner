## 1. Overview

411 Scanner investigates suspicious content you submit and returns an AI research report. This statement explains what data we collect, why, and what we do with it. Contact: **grindmasterfire@gmail.com**

## 2. Data We Collect

**You provide:**
- **Scan submissions** — images, screenshots, links, and text you submit for investigation.
- **Account information** — if you sign in with Google: your Google account identifier, email address, and display name (provided by Google; we never see your password).

**Collected automatically:**
- **Usage data** — scans performed, features used, error events (including our internal diagnostic codes), and aggregate performance data.
- **Device data** — device model, OS version, app version, and advertising identifiers used for ad delivery and measurement.
- **Purchase data** — subscription status and product IDs, processed by Google Play (we do not see or store your payment details).

## 3. How We Use It

- **Provide the service:** run investigations, deliver reports, enforce scan credits, and manage subscriptions.
- **Improve detection:** anonymized intelligence from scans is retained so repeat scams are recognized faster for everyone (our Cache Bank). This is how a scam reported by one user protects the next.
- **Analytics and reliability:** understand usage, diagnose errors, and keep the service running.
- **Advertising:** AdMob may use advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads per its own privacy policy.

We **do not sell your personal information** to anyone.

## 4. Data Sharing

We share data only as needed to operate the service:

- **Google Cloud / Firebase** — backend infrastructure, authentication, and storage (data processor).
- **Google AdMob** — ad delivery and measurement.
- **Google Play** — billing and subscription management.
- **Authorities** — only where required by law.

We do not share scan content or personal data with data brokers, marketers, or unrelated third parties.

## 5. Data Retention

- Scan intelligence may be retained in anonymized form to improve fraud detection.
- Account and billing records are retained as required for business and legal purposes.
- Diagnostic logs are retained for a limited period and then deleted or anonymized.

## 6. Your Rights & Choices

- **Access and correction:** contact us to request a copy or correction of your personal data.
- **Account deletion:** you may delete your account and associated personal data at any time — contact **grindmasterfire@gmail.com** from the email address on the account, and we will delete it. Anonymized fraud-intelligence records that cannot identify you may be retained.
- **Opt out of personalization:** use your device's ad settings to limit ad tracking (this does not remove ads from the free tier).
- **Guests:** guest sessions collect minimal data; signing in links prior guest activity to your account.

## 7. Children's Privacy

411 Scanner is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards (encrypted transport, authenticated access, least-privilege backend access). No system is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Changes

We may update this statement as the service evolves. Material changes will be announced in the app or at our contact address. Continued use after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.

## 10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: **grindmasterfire@gmail.com**
